# Using the Forum Mini Feed Widget

The Forum Mini Feed widget allows you to display a feed of forum posts from an entire forum or from specific categories. This widget is found in the Advanced & Setup section of the Insert Palette.

## Adding the Forum Mini Feed Widget

1. **Edit a Page**:
    - Open the page where you want to add the Forum Mini Feed widget.

2. **Insert the Widget**:
    - Click the **Insert** button.
    - Navigate to the **Advanced & Setup** section.
    - Drag the **Forum Mini Feed** widget <span class="icon-widget"><img src="/admin/graphics/adminthemeassets.php?show=widgets&name=Forum_Mini_Feed"></span> to the desired location on your page.

## Configuring the Forum Mini Feed Widget

To configure the Forum Mini Feed widget, right-click on the widget or select it and click the ellipses button to open the properties menu.

1. **Tag**:
   - Select the primary forum tag or a specific category tag to display posts from.
2. **Number of pages to show**:
   - Set the number of forum posts to display in the feed.
3. **Show meta info**:
   - Enable this option to display metadata information such as the post author and date.

By following these steps and configuring these options, you can customize the Forum Mini Feed widget to display relevant forum posts in a specific area of your website.
