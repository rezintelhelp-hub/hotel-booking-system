# Using the Copyright Notice widget

The Copyright Notice widget is used to display a copyright notice on your website, with the year automatically displaying the current year. This widget helps keep your copyright information up to date without needing manual updates each year.

## Adding a Copyright Notice Widget

1. **Open the Insert Palette**:
   - Click the **+** button to open the Insert Palette.
   - Drag the Copyright Notice widget <span class="icon-widget"><img src="/admin/graphics/adminthemeassets.php?show=widgets&name=Copyright_Notice"></span> to the desired location on the page. It is typically added to a sitewide content area, such as the footer.

## Adding Content

The Copyright Notice widget includes an editable zone called **Company Name** where you can enter your company name as a text item. The process for adding text is the same as with other editable zones in widgets.

1. **Enter Company Name**:
   - Once the Copyright Notice widget is added to the page, locate the **Company Name** editable zone within the widget.
   - To add content, drag items into the **Company Name** editable zone, either directly from the Insert Palette or from elsewhere in the editor. Refer to [working with text](#working-with-text) for detailed instructions on how to add and format text items.
2. **Adding a Link** (Optional):
   - You can optionally add a link to your company name in the normal way, and it will be preserved in the output of the widget.

By following these steps, you can effectively use the Copyright Notice widget to display up-to-date copyright information on your website, ensuring your company name and current year are always correctly shown.
