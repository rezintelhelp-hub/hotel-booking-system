# Stripe Payment Method Widget

The Stripe Payment Method widget is found in the **Advanced & Setup** section of the Insert Palette. This widget allows your customers to create a billing account in Stripe without leaving your website.

## Adding the Stripe Payment Method Widget

1. **Open the Insert Palette**:
   - Click the **+** button to open the Insert Palette.
   - Navigate to the **Advanced & Setup** section.
   - Drag the **Stripe Payment Method** widget <span class="icon-widget"><img src="/admin/graphics/adminthemeassets.php?show=widgets&name=Stripe_Payment_Method"></span> to the desired location on the page.

## Configuring the Stripe Payment Method Widget

1. **Opening the Properties Menu**:
   - To access the properties menu, right-click the widget or select the widget and click the ellipses button (**…**).
2. **Properties**:
   - **No payment method text**  
     Customize the message displayed to customers before they add a payment method.
   - **Has payment method text**  
     Customize the message displayed to customers after they add a payment method.

## Setting Up Your Website

This widget should be placed in an area that only logged-in customers can see, such as a welcome page or user account page.

To set up your website for using the Stripe Subscription Product widgets, follow these steps:

1. **Add the Stripe Payment Method widget** to an area of your site only visible to logged-in users.
2. **Add the Stripe Webhook widget** to a public part of your site.
3. **Enter your Stripe credentials** in **Settings** > **Stripe**.

For more details on setting up your Stripe Subscription Product widget, refer to [Stripe Subscription Product Widget](#stripe-subscription-product).

For more details on setting up your Stripe Webhook widget, refer to [Stripe Webhook Widget](#stripe-webhook).
