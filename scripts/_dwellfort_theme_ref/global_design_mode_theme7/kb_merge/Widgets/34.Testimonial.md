# Using the Testimonial Widget

The Testimonial widget is a handy tool for adding one or more testimonials to your page, enhancing its credibility and visual appeal.

## Adding a Testimonial Widget

1. **Edit the Page**:
   - Open the page you want to edit.
2. **Open the Insert Palette**:
   - Click the **+** button to open the Insert Palette.
   - Navigate to the **Text** section.
   - Drag the **Testimonial** widget <span class="icon-widget"><img src="/admin/graphics/adminthemeassets.php?show=widgets&name=Testimonial"></span> to the desired location in your content area.

## Adding Content to the Testimonial Widget

1. **Add Testimonial Text**:
   - Click or drag text for the testimonial into the **Text** editable zone.
2. **Add Author Name**:
   - Add the testimonial author's name to the **Author** editable zone.
   - You can use bold, italics, and other formatting options to add emphasis or additional information, such as a location.

### Example

For example:
- **Text**: "This product has significantly improved my workflow and efficiency."
- **Author**: *John Doe, New York*

By following these steps, you can effectively use the Testimonial widget to display testimonials on your website, making it more engaging and trustworthy for your visitors.
