# Using the Geolocation Content Widget

The Geolocation Content widget allows you to display different content based on the visitor's country. This widget is found in the Advanced & Setup section of the Insert Palette.

## Adding the Geolocation Content Widget

1. **Edit a Page**:
   - Open the page where you want to add the Geolocation Content widget.
2. **Insert the Widget**:
   - Click the **Insert** button.
   - Navigate to the **Advanced & Setup** section.
   - Drag the **Geolocation Content** widget <span class="icon-widget"><img src="/admin/graphics/adminthemeassets.php?show=widgets&name=Geolocation_Content"></span> to the desired location on your page.

## Configuring the Geolocation Content Widget

To configure the Geolocation Content widget, right-click on the widget or select it and click the ellipses button to open the properties menu.

1. **Valid country codes**:
   - Enter the two-letter country codes that you want to restrict the content to. Separate multiple codes with commas.

## Editable Zones

The Geolocation Content widget contains two editable zones:

1. **Valid Content**:
   - This content will be shown to users who match the country codes entered in the properties menu.
2. **Invalid Content**:
   - This content will be shown to users who do not match the country codes entered in the properties menu.

By configuring the country codes and adding content to the appropriate editable zones, you can tailor the displayed content based on the visitor's location.
