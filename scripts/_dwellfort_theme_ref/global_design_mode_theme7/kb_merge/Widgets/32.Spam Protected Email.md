# Using the Spam Protected Email Widget

The Spam Protected Email widget allows you to display an email address on your website in a way that protects it from being harvested by spambots. This ensures that your email address remains accessible to your visitors while reducing the risk of spam.

## Adding the Spam Protected Email Widget

1. **Open the Insert Palette**:
   - Click the **+** button to open the Insert Palette.
   - Navigate to the **Page Feature** section.
   - Drag the **Spam Protected Email** widget <span class="icon-widget"><img src="/admin/graphics/adminthemeassets.php?show=widgets&name=Spam_Protected_Email"></span> to the desired location on the page.

## Configuration Options

1. **Email Address to Protect**:
   - Enter the email address you wish to display. The widget will automatically obfuscate it to protect against spambots.
2. **Clickable Text**:
   - Customize the text that will be displayed as the clickable link. For example, you can use "Contact Us" or "Email Support".
3. **Text Before Link**:
   - Enter any text you want to appear before the clickable email link.
4. **Text After Link**:
   - Enter any text you want to appear after the clickable email link.
5. **Subject**:
   - Optionally, specify a default subject for the email. This subject will be automatically filled in when the user clicks the email link.
6. **Show Icon**:
   - Toggle this option to display an email icon alongside the clickable text.

By following these steps, you can effectively use the Spam Protected Email widget to display your email address securely on your website, ensuring that it is accessible to your visitors while minimizing the risk of spam.
