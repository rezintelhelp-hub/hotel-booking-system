# Shop Category Content Widget

The Shop Category Content widget, found in the Advanced & Setup section of the insert palette, allows you to create sections of the shop that only display when a certain category is selected.

## Adding the Shop Category Content Widget

1. **Open the insert palette**
   - Click the plus icon (+) to open the insert palette.
2. **Select the Shop Category Content widget**
   - Drag the Shop Category Content widget <span class="icon-widget"><img src="/admin/graphics/adminthemeassets.php?show=widgets&name=Shop_Category_Content"></span> from the Advanced & Setup section into the editor.
3. **Open the properties menu**
   - Right-click the Shop Category Content widget or click on it and then click the ellipsis (three dots) button to open the properties menu.

## Configuring the Shop Category Content Widget

1. **Category**
   - Manually enter the name of the category for which this content should be displayed. Ensure the category name matches exactly with the category used in your shop system.

## Editable Zones

- **Content**
  - Add the content that should be displayed when the specified category is selected.

This widget is useful for providing additional information or promotional content that is relevant only to certain categories within your shop, ensuring a more personalized and relevant shopping experience for your visitors.
