# Using the Social Links Widget

The Social Links widget allows you to display social media icons on your website, providing easy access for visitors to your social profiles. This widget includes various customization options to fit your design needs.

## Adding the Social Links Widget

1. **Open the Insert Palette**:
   - Click the **+** button to open the Insert Palette.
   - Navigate to the **Page Feature** section.
   - Drag the **Social Links** widget <span class="icon-widget"><img src="/admin/graphics/adminthemeassets.php?show=widgets&name=Social_Links"></span> to the desired location on the page.

## Configuration Options

1. **Align**:
   - Choose the alignment of the icons: left, center, or right.
2. **Style**:
   - Change the graphical style of the icons using the Style property.
3. **Size**:
   - Switch between small or large icons.
4. **Show Phone**:
   - Toggle to display the phone icon.
5. **Show Email**:
   - Toggle to display the email icon.
6. **Allow Wrapping**:
   - Enable this option to allow following content to wrap next to the icons.

## Setting Social Links

- **Settings**:
  - The links for the social icons are set in the main **Settings > Social Links** area. Ensure you configure these links to direct visitors to your social media profiles.

By following these steps, you can effectively use the Social Links widget to enhance your website's connectivity with your social media profiles, offering visitors quick access to your social channels.
