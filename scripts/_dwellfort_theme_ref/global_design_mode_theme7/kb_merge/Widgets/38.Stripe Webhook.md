# Stripe Webhook Widget

The Stripe Webhook widget is found in the **Advanced & Setup** section of the Insert Palette. This widget is essential for setting up your website to use the Stripe Subscription Product widgets.

## Adding the Stripe Webhook Widget

1. **Open the Insert Palette**:
   - Click the **+** button to open the Insert Palette.
   - Navigate to the **Advanced & Setup** section.
   - Drag the **Stripe Webhook** widget <span class="icon-widget"><img src="/admin/graphics/adminthemeassets.php?show=widgets&name=Stripe_Webhook"></span> to the desired location on the page.

## Configuring the Stripe Webhook Widget

1. **Opening the Properties Menu**:
   - To access the properties menu, right-click the widget or select the widget and click the ellipses button (**…**).

## Setting Up Your Website

You need to add one instance of this widget per website to a publicly accessible page or part of a page. Ensure that the page is published and not within a protected area.

To set up your website for using the Stripe Subscription Product widgets, follow these steps:

1. **Add the Stripe Payment Method widget** to an area of your site only visible to logged-in users.
2. **Add the Stripe Webhook widget** to a public part of your site.
3. **Enter your Stripe credentials** in **Settings** > **Stripe**.

By following these steps, you can effectively use the Stripe Subscription Product widgets to offer subscription products on your website.

For more details on setting up your Stripe Subscription Product widget, refer to [Stripe Subscription Product Widget](#stripe-subscription-product).

For more details on setting up your Stripe Payment Method widget, refer to [Stripe Payment Method Widget](#stripe-payment-method).
