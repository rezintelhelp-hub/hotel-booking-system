# Google Maps Widget

The Google Maps widget is found in the **Page Feature** section of the Insert Palette. This widget allows you to display a map of a specific location on your website.

## Adding the Google Maps Widget

1. **Open the Insert Palette**:
   - Click the **+** button to open the Insert Palette.
   - Navigate to the **Page Feature** section.
   - Drag the **Google Maps** widget <span class="icon-widget"><img src="/admin/graphics/adminthemeassets.php?show=widgets&name=Google_Maps_SS9"></span> to your desired location on the page.

## Configuring the Google Maps Widget

To access the properties menu, right-click the widget or select the widget and click the ellipses button (**…**).

1. **Address**:
   - Enter the address for the location you want to display on the map.

**Note**: If the [Consent Banner](#consent-banner) is enabled, the map will not be displayed until the visitor accepts functional cookies.
