# Using the YouTube Embed Widget

The YouTube Embed widget allows you to easily embed YouTube videos on your page, providing a seamless way to share video content with your visitors.

## Adding a YouTube Embed Widget

1. **Edit the Page**:
   - Open the page you want to edit.

2. **Open the Insert Palette**:
   - Click the **+** button to open the Insert Palette.
   - Navigate to the **Page Features** section.
   - Drag the **YouTube Embed** widget <span class="icon-widget"><img src="/admin/graphics/adminthemeassets.php?show=widgets&name=YouTube_Embed"></span> to the desired location on the page.

## Configuring the YouTube Embed Widget

1. **Open the Properties Menu**:
   - Right-click on the widget, or
   - Single-click the widget and then click the ellipsis button (three dots) that appears.
2. **YouTube Link**:
   - Enter the link to the YouTube video in the **YouTube Link** field.
3. **Title**:
   - Add a title for the video in the **Title** field. This title is used for accessibility purposes and is not normally visible to users.

By following these steps, you can effectively use the YouTube Embed widget to display YouTube videos on your website, enhancing the multimedia content available to your visitors.
