# Using the Video Widget

The Video widget is a powerful tool that allows you to embed a video player on your page. It offers several customization options, including the ability to display content to the visitor after they have watched the entire video.

## Adding the Video Widget

1. **Open the Insert Palette**:
   - Click the **+** button to open the Insert Palette.
   - Navigate to the **Media & Files** section.
   - Drag the **Video** widget <span class="icon-widget"><img src="/admin/graphics/adminthemeassets.php?show=widgets&name=Video"></span> to the desired location on the page.

## Configuring the Video Widget

To configure the Video widget, right-click on the widget or single-click the widget and then click the ellipsis button (three dots) that appears. Then, adjust the following properties as needed:

1. **Video**  
   - Use the **Video** option to choose a video from storage. You can also upload a video directly here.
2. **Quick-Add Thumbnail**  
   - Use the **Quick-add thumbnail** option to upload a thumbnail for the video if one wasn't already attached in storage.
3. **Size**  
   - Use the **Size** option to adjust the size of the video player.
4. **Hide Video Once Seen**  
   - Enable the **Hide video once seen** option to hide the video after the visitor has watched it.
5. **Allow Play Again**  
   - Enable the **Allow play again** option to allow the visitor to replay the video.
6. **Alignment**  
   - Use the **Alignment** option to set the alignment of the video (left, center, right).
7. **Logged In Only**  
   - Tick the **Logged in only** option to make the video visible only if the visitor is logged in.

By following these steps, you can effectively use the Video widget to add and customize videos on your website, enhancing the user experience.
