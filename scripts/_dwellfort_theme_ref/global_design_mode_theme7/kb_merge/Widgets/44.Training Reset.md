# Training Reset Widget

The Training Reset widget allows users to reset their progress in an online training course, including all their answers and results. They will then be able to take the course again.

## Configuring the Training Reset Widget

To configure the Training Reset Widget, you need to open the properties menu. To open the properties menu, either right-click on the widget or click on the widget and then click the ellipses button.

1. **Reset test groups**
   - Enter the tag(s) of the test groups that should be reset when this button is used.
2. **Redirect on reset link**
   - Choose a page that the user will be taken to when they retake the test.
3. **Reset button label**
   - Enter the text to display on the reset button.
4. **Reset videos viewed**
   - Enable this option to reset the record of videos viewed by the user.
