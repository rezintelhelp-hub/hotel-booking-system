# Social and Contact Settings

The **Social and Contact** settings area in SetSeed allows you to enter links to your social media profiles, as well as your contact information such as phone number and email. These values are used by the template for modules and widgets that display social links and contact details on your website.

## Accessing Social and Contact Settings

1. Click the blue home icon in the main menu.
2. Select **Settings** from the menu.
3. Navigate to the **Social and Contact** section in the sidebar.

## Configuring Social and Contact Information

1. **Social Media Links**:
   - Enter the URLs for your social media profiles. These links will be used by modules and widgets to display social media icons and links on your website. Common social media platforms include:
     - Facebook
     - Twitter
     - LinkedIn
     - Instagram
     - YouTube
     - Pinterest
2. **Contact Information**:
   - **Phone Number**: Enter your business phone number.
   - **Email Address**: Enter your business email address.

## Summary

By configuring these settings, you can ensure that your social media links and contact information are displayed correctly on your website. These values are utilized by various modules and widgets in your template, providing visitors with easy access to your social media profiles and contact details.
