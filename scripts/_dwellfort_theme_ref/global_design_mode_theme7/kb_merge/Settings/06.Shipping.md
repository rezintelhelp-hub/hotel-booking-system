# Shipping Settings

The **Shipping** settings area in SetSeed allows you to configure address autocomplete functionality and define per-order shipping costs. These settings help streamline the checkout process and manage shipping charges based on the customer's location and the items in their basket.

## Accessing Shipping Settings

1. Click the blue home icon in the main menu.
2. Select **Settings** from the menu.
3. Navigate to the **Shipping** section in the sidebar.

## Configuring Address Autocomplete

The first two options in the Shipping settings relate to the Address Autocomplete functionality, which uses Google to autocomplete the visitor's address on the checkout page.

1. **Enable Address Autocomplete**:
   - Tick the **Enable Address Autocomplete** option to allow Google to autocomplete addresses on the checkout page.
2. **Autocomplete Countries**:
   - Use the **Autocomplete Countries** field to specify the countries where you want the address autocomplete functionality to work. You can leave this blank to allow autocomplete for all countries.

## Defining Per-Order Shipping Costs

To enable and configure per-order shipping costs, follow these steps:

1. **Enable Per-Order Shipping Costs**:
   - Tick the **Add Per-Order Shipping Costs** option to enable this feature.
2. **Shipping Name**:
   - Enter a label in the **Shipping Name** field that customers will see during checkout.
3. **Hide Unless Currency Match Region**:
   - Tick the **Hide Unless Currency Match Region** option if you want to hide shipping options when the user's session currency does not match the default currency set in the shop settings.

## Configuring Shipping Tiers

The shipping calculation is divided into five tiers, each with ten options. Only one tier will be displayed to the customer based on the total amount, weight, or volume of the items in their basket.

1. **Defining Tier Thresholds**:
   - At the start of each tier, you will find fields to define the threshold for the amount, weight, and volume. Leave these fields blank if you do not want to use them as triggers to switch between tiers.
2. **Configuring Shipping Options**:
   - Each tier can have up to ten options. For each option, you need to configure the following:
     - **Name**: Enter a name for the shipping option.
     - **Price**: Specify the shipping cost.
     - **Free Code**: Optionally, enter a free code. If the customer enters this code in the coupon field on the checkout page, the shipping will become free.
     - **Charge Sales Tax**: Tick this box if you want to charge sales tax on the entire order based on the shipping option selected by the customer. For example, you may not be required to collect sales tax for orders sent to countries other than your own. In such cases, you can add an international shipping option and untick the Charge Sales Tax option. This ensures that when users choose that shipping option, they will not be charged sales tax on their order.

## Summary

By configuring these settings, you can effectively manage how shipping costs are calculated and displayed to your customers. The Address Autocomplete functionality improves the user experience by simplifying address entry, while the tiered shipping options provide flexibility in handling shipping charges based on various criteria. This ensures a streamlined and efficient checkout process for your online store.
