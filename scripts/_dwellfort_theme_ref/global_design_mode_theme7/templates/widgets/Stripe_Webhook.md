The **Stripe Webhook** widget is part of setting up your website for using the ‘Stripe Subscription Product’ widgets.

You need to add one instance of this widget per website, to a publicly accessible page or part of a page. i.e don't add it to the protected area of a ‘Logged In Only Container’ or to a protected template section. Ensure your page is published.

There are three parts to setting up your website for using the Stripe Subscription Product widgets: 

1. Adding the ‘Stripe Payment Method’ widget to an area of your site only visible to logged in users. The ‘Stripe Payment Method’ has its own help information for more detail.
2. Adding this **Stripe Webhook** widget to a public part of your site. 
3. Adding your Stripe credentials in Settings > Stripe.
