The **Stripe Subscription Product** widget lets you setup a product on your site that charges an amount on a regular, repeating basis.

You can configure the product using the properties menu once you've added it to a page. You can learn more about each option by reading the explanation when clicking each one.

Only logged in users with an active Stripe payment method can subscribe to your products.

There are three parts to setting up your website for using the **Stripe Subscription Product** widgets: 

1. Adding the ‘Stripe Payment Method’ widget to an area of your site only visible to logged in users. The ‘Stripe Payment Method’ has its own help information for more detail.
2. Adding the ‘Stripe Webhook’ widget to a public part of your site. 
3. Adding your Stripe credentials in Settings > Stripe.

You can then add these Stripe Subscription Product widgets to your site to add one or more subscription products.
