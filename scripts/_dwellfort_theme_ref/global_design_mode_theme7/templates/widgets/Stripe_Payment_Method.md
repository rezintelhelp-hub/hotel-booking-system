The **Stripe Payment Method** widget is needed as part of setting up your site to use the ‘Stripe Subscription Product’ widgets.

The **Stripe Payment Method** widget is what lets your customers create a billing account in Stripe (all without leaving your website or being aware of Stripe). This attaches a Stripe payment method to them within their account so they can then subscribe to one or more products on your site. 

This widget needs to be in an area that only logged in customers can see, for example at the top of a welcome page or user account page.

There are three parts to setting up your website for using the ‘Stripe Subscription Product’ widgets: 

1. Adding this Payment Method widget to an area of your site only visible to logged in users.
2. Adding the ‘Stripe Webhook’ widget to a public part of your site. The Stripe Webhook widget has its own help information for more detail.
3. Adding your Stripe credentials in Settings > Stripe.
