You can use this widget to request permission for all of the consent purposes you have setup in Settings > Consent widget setup.

It's ideal on an area of your site like a privacy policy page as it lets the visitor review all the consent purposes on your site.

The widget simply lists your consent purposes along with a tickbox for each one. Once the user has confirmed they are happy with a given consent purpose, that choice will reflect anyone on the site that uses a Consent Container to show content based on the same purpose.
