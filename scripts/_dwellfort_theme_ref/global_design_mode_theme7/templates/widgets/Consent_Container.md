The Consent Container widget wraps elements on your page in a wrapper than ensures they only get displayed if the visitor has ticked the correct consent box.

You can setup different consent purposes in Settings > Consent widget setup. Then you can configure an instance of this widget on your page to look for one of your consent purposes before displaying its contents.

If the user hasn't agreed to a consent purpose, they will see a message and the tickbox instead of the content you add to the widget.
